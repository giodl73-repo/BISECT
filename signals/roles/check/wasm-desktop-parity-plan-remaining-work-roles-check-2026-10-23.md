## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 9. P1 blockers: 0. P2 issues: 6. P3 notes: 21.
Verdict: **APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS for the amended implementation plan only**.
All six P2 findings now have concrete planning amendments. Their corresponding
matrix, reconciliation, practitioner and release obligations remain open.

Top finding: replace the accumulating historical checkpoints with a clear current
handoff that requires runnable, source-backed acceptance inputs before new support
claims. BENCHMARK, COVENANT, SURVEY and TRENCH agree that evidence chronology and
claim scope must stay explicit. CONTOUR and LEDGER agree that acquisition planning
is not a completed source lifecycle. CANVASS and SURVEY agree that practitioner
actions need an explicit inventory rather than a general promise of toolkit parity.

## Three amendments applied

1. Mark delivered Verify and its earlier pending state explicitly; preserve earlier
   review records and distinguish the current handoff from historical checkpoints.
2. Define the next Fetch/FLETCH table and predeclared real-data manifest, with
   native authority, inputs, expected outcomes, runtime/API requirements and owners.
3. Require a practitioner action inventory, retain certification/authority semantics,
   and bind future acceptance to fresh affected gates, final package and immutable
   evidence without upgrading unresolved parents.

## Verification and limits

Reviewed installed role profiles, plan, ledger priorities, shared Fetch planner,
browser acquisition eligibility and the scoped continuation review. Local Markdown
links and changed-file whitespace were checked after amendment. No engine rebuild,
browser/native run, live-provider check, deployment or full-parity acceptance was
performed for this documentation-only review. Historical runtime evidence is cited
as recorded, not reverified here. Existing PP-25 and PP-32 remain the applicable
pitfall families; this review introduces no new runtime failure mechanism or solved
pitfall claim.
