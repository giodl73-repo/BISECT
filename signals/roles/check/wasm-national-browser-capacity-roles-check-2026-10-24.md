## Synthesis and three amendments

Roles reviewed: 7. P1 blockers: 0. P2 findings: 4, closed within the bounded local
scope. P3 conditions: 17, addressed with explicit limits or carried to their named
parent/host/source/release obligations.

Verdict: **APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS** for the scoped local preview. No capability
parent or acceptance-v1 bundle is marked verified by these role perspectives.

Top finding: aggregate workflow limits can strand valid engine outputs. SURVEY,
BENCHMARK, LEDGER and TRENCH agree that a complete Run requires retained results,
usable backups/exports and explicit recovery, with actual browser evidence.

Applied amendments:

1. Align bounded live receipts, typed archive complexity and operation-specific
   RCTX reading, with actual full-roster and large-context regressions.
2. Preserve exact results while versioning and compressing the larger ZIP export;
   independently validate all original state archives and accepted sources.
3. Freeze exact artifacts and failed attempts, preserve historical source versions,
   and register only scoped children while retaining parent/host/source conditions.
