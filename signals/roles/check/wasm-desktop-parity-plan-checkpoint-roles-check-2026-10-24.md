## Synthesis

Roles reviewed: 7. P1 blockers: 0. P2 issues: 4. P3 notes: 17.

Verdict: **APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS for implementation planning only.** Three P2 planning clarifications are addressed by amendments; C1 remains an explicit evidence-closure condition. This does not close any runtime or release condition. Historical review IDs retain their original scopes.

Top finding: the remaining national work is an actual browser workflow acceptance task, even though all 50 states already match the independent CLI in both direct WASM profiles. SURVEY, BENCHMARK, LEDGER and TRENCH agree that scheduling, saved continuation and map/export outcomes require their own evidence.

## Three amendments applied

1. Add a governing current checkpoint and correct stale national/large-context status headers, preserving historical handoffs and accepted artifacts.
2. Fix the next national browser acceptance contract: full roster/native comparisons, observed concurrency, completed-prefix cancellation, inert Open, explicit verification, unfinished-only continuation, archives and a separately declared national map.
3. Keep correction-package evidence closure before support registration, then broader acquisition/derivation, lifecycle and real-host acceptance. Preserve ownership and the existing acceptance ledger.
