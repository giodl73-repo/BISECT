## Synthesis and amendments

Eight perspectives, 25 findings: zero P1, four P2 closed for this local scope,
21 P3 checks/residual boundaries. Verdict: **APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS** for the
scoped local package. The remaining host, native orchestration, formal bundle,
source-authenticity and release/full-parity boundaries remain open.

Top issue: final user-visible bytes must be the tested bytes. BENCHMARK/COVENANT/
TRENCH agree on artifact binding; SURVEY/LEDGER require the native-consumable
end-to-end journey. CANVASS/TALLY/VAULT retain source and authority boundaries.

Three amendments implemented or required:

1. Separate original source bytes, execution request and prepared-result hashes;
   freeze native references and served browser bytes instead of borrowed identities.
2. Complete the reviewed UI, registry-last/readback and exact continuation contract,
   with concise completion status and an explicit full receipt download.
3. Gate both actual engines and one new final package; integrate scoped ledger
   children, preserve historical evidence and retain a content-addressed immutable
   snapshot with preflight capacity refusal and a completed manifest only after verification.
