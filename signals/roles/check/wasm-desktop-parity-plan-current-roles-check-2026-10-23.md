## Synthesis and amendments

Roles reviewed: **7**. Findings: **21** — **0 P1**, **3 P2** (all amended in
the plan), **18 P3** remaining acceptance obligations.

Verdict: **APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS for continued implementation**.
The plan is sufficiently specific to proceed; it is not release or full-parity
approval. No runtime suite was started by this documentation review.

The top finding is that the plan's next action must reflect the actual integrated
backend rather than repeat completed feasibility work. MERIDIAN/TRENCH/SURVEY
agree on current-state wording and bounded next work; BENCHMARK/COVENANT require
final-artifact evidence rather than treating a passing local runner as release.

Three amendments applied:

1. Replaced stale backend/ordering/checkpoint language with a historical-to-current
   account and linked scoped integration evidence, retaining all broader gaps.
2. Required affected legacy gates on both actual build variants, separate loaded-C
   and final packaged-worker checks, and packaged dependency notices. Local-data
   prerequisites remain explicitly integration-tier work.
3. Defined the next WP-03 slice: selected-root or archive discovery, one reviewed
   run, accepted outputs, installation/download, and explicit recovery. It reuses
   existing adapters and excludes reset/force, broader queues and national scale.
