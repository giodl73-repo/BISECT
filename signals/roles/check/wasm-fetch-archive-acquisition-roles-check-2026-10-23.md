## Synthesis and amendments

Roles reviewed: 8. P1 blockers: 0. P2 issues: 4 closed for this scoped package.
P3 notes: 20 carried or retained as explicit boundaries.
Verdict: **APPROVED-WITH-CONDITIONS for the local archive workflow preview**.

Top finding: downloaded/extracted bytes and a completion marker must not imply
an existing planned target or implemented downstream spatial join. CONTOUR,
MERIDIAN and SURVEY agree on separate stage outcomes. BENCHMARK and COVENANT
require actual native/raw/final-worker evidence and preservation of failed
attempts. LEDGER and VAULT require local receipt/authority semantics to stay honest.

Three amendments applied:

1. Separate target presence from native extraction marker, preserve real archive
   members and shared zone-join limitations, and add PP-33 with regression links.
2. Support explicit local import and single-copy cache/project handoff without
   fictitious HTTP attempts, imported authority or broadened private URL access.
3. Bind the final v2 package to both-build/native/GUI evidence and a new immutable
   snapshot; keep failed initial acceptance and all broader obligations visible.

Remaining conditions include full source/CLI/streaming lifecycle, EIA-provider
and workbook coverage, shared zone joins, fresh derivation and larger successful
matrix workloads, practitioner inventory, notices/custody and release acceptance.
This review neither deploys nor promotes any full-parity parent to verified.
