**Date:** 2026-07-10

**Posture:** Internal model-statute review; not legal advice or legislative

## Architecture Change

The model bill no longer requires adoption of an algorithmic final map. It
requires:

- a block-level, precommitted geographic benchmark;
- a lawful final plan chosen by the authorized State body or court;
- a complete baseline-to-final record;
- mandatory federal voting-rights compliance;
- public alternatives, review, and reasons; and
- technical and substantive challenge paths.

introduction-ready statutory text, enacted law, or an externally reviewed legal
opinion. Effectiveness is expressly gated on block-level technical readiness.
