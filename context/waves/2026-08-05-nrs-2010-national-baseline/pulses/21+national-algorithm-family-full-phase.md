# Pulse 21 - National Algorithm-Family Full Phase

## Authorization and scope

The exactly regenerated eight-State pilot authorized the already frozen full
phase of protocol `neutral-algorithm-family-national-bakeoff-v1`. The governed
sequence retained the pilot order and then ran every remaining multi-district
2020 congressional State by increasing seat count and State code. The six
at-large States remain design exclusions. The full matrix contains 44 States,
four structures per State, and 176 cells.

## Clean-run result

The governed full matrix passed 44 of 44 States and 176 of 176 cells. Every
native audit and population check passed, manifest population flags agreed
with audit certificates, and each State preserved the frozen common inputs.
No cell required a seed retry.

Across the 264 within-State structure pairs, maximum-overlap assignment
agreement ranged from 47.6257 percent to 100 percent. The 132 alternative
edge-cut ratios to standard-bisect ranged from 0.623301 to 5.441826. These are
descriptive within-State mechanics. Raw cuts are not pooled, and neither range
identifies a generally best structure.

## Evidence gate and decision

An independent execution regenerated all 176 native cells and the verifier
passed the normalized derived outputs exactly. During that gate, fresh-process
probes exposed nondeterministic recursive METIS scheduling, boundary-rebalance
tie selection, and floating summation order. Stable recursive scheduling,
global-vertex tie breaking, and canonical edge ordering corrected the
assignment-affecting paths. Five independent California standard-bisect probes
and five New Mexico ratio-optimal-area probes then produced one canonical
assignment hash per case.

Edge cuts are research-reporting values normalized to five decimal places.
That precision makes a native floating accumulation-order difference below
the declared reporting resolution non-semantic; canonical assignments remain
byte-compared. The independent package and the published package have the same
normalized `analysis.json` SHA-256,
`abd91ba44c1092aeafc6d0e632f1ca6849ca857acb348ca2147a3d6910aaf483`
(case-insensitive hexadecimal).

The gate therefore establishes complete frozen-cohort implementation validity
for this repository's 44-State tract cohort. It does not establish
partisan fairness, geometric compactness, VRA or legal compliance, causal
effects, or an algorithm ranking.
