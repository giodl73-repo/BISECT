## Completion Definition

For every State and Census year:

1. every canonical Census unit is assigned exactly once;
2. no gaps, overlaps, omitted islands, or duplicate units exist;
3. the enacted floor/ceiling recursive seat schedule is followed;
4. each child remains connected under the published island rule;
5. population and connectivity claims are independently verified;
6. weighted-boundary and canonical objectives are either independently proved
   or explicitly reported as unresolved exact-proof gaps;
7. every timeout, infeasibility, and unresolved gap is public; and
8. final RPLAN/RCTX/tree/proof/manifest packages verify.
