# Small States 2020 Certification

## Mission

Produce complete, replayable 2020 wall-to-wall packages for the smallest
States, while reporting exact population, boundary, and canonical proof status
separately.

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Baseline | Target | Actual | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| One-district State packages | 0 | 6 | 6 | DONE |
| Wall-to-wall unit coverage | unreported | 100% in every published package | 100% | DONE |
| Positive and negative verifier coverage | partial | every active package | complete | DONE |
| Two-district complete roots | 0 | at least 1 or a published exact blocker | 2 | DONE |
| Proof scaling report | Rhode Island only | all attempted small States | 10 States | DONE |

## Fixed Invariants

- blocks remain the normative 2020 unit;
- no unit may be omitted, duplicated, or assigned twice;
- island bridges follow the published deterministic rule;
- population and connectivity are independently checked;
- heuristic incumbents are never labeled exact;
- boundary and canonical claims require independent proofs; and
- unresolved proof gaps are published rather than blocking wall-to-wall output.

## Claim Boundary

This wave establishes operational wall-to-wall certification and measures exact
objective proof coverage. It does not imply universal block-level boundary
optimality.

## Lessons

- arithmetic population lower bounds avoid unnecessary proof logs;
- node-specific METIS seed screening is required for recursive population
  balance;
- operational coverage scales substantially farther than exact boundary proof;
  and
- national execution must publish these two completion dimensions separately.
