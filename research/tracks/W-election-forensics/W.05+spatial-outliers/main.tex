\begin{abstract}
Neighboring reporting units often resemble one another because they share local
geography, administration, demography, and political context. A reporting unit
that diverges sharply from its geographic neighbors can therefore be a useful
candidate for review. It is not, by itself, evidence of fraud, proof of error,
or certification evidence. This W.05 method paper instantiates the W.01
forensic report contract for spatial outliers: neighbors are defined by shared
boundaries, features are derived from hash-bound RCOUNT reporting units and
geography, scores may come from local Moran's I/LISA statistics, spatial-lag
residuals, or neighbor-difference z-scores, and every output remains an
investigation queue. The paper defines the method, input contract, intended
fixture roles, and false-positive boundary. It does not claim a shipped
forensics crate, function, fixture, or test.
\end{abstract}
