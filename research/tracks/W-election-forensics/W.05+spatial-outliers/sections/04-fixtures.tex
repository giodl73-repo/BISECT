\section{Fixture Pattern}

The intended W.05 fixtures follow the four-role W-series pattern. They are
specified here as design requirements, not as shipped tests.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Fixture & Role \\
\midrule
No-anomaly smooth field & A synthetic package where neighboring units vary smoothly. The method should return low scores or an empty low-priority queue. \\
Injected spatial outlier & A package with one planted unit that sharply departs from its neighbors. The method should surface that unit near the top of the queue. \\
Innocent discontinuity & A package with a real boundary discontinuity that looks unusual but is known to be benign, exercising the false-positive caveat. \\
Explained demographic edge & A boundary fixture where a sharp neighbor difference is fully explained by a known demographic edge, such as an urban-rural or college-town boundary. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

These fixtures test two behaviors at once. The method should notice a planted
local anomaly, and the report should refuse to convert an explainable spatial
edge into a fraud claim. The false-positive fixtures are therefore not secondary;
they are the part of the method that protects the investigative boundary.
