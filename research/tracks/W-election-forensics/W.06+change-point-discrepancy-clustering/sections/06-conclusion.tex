\section{Conclusion}

This paper closes the W-series family survey opened by W.01 by specifying the
last two atlas families: change-point tests over reporting sequences and
clustering tests over audit discrepancies. Both families fit the same discipline
as the rest of the track. They consume hash-bound RCOUNT inputs, emit a common
forensic report shape, and attach caveats to every score.

The result is a method contract, not a claim of implementation. Later work may
place these analytics in RCOUNT audit-style reports or in a dedicated
forensics crate. Whatever the placement, the boundary is fixed: the output is
investigative, not certifying; descriptive, not causal; and useful only as a
prioritized invitation for further review.
