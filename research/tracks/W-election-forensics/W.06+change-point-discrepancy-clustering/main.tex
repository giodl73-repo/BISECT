\begin{abstract}
Change-point tests and audit-discrepancy clustering are investigative election
forensic methods. They can prioritize review by flagging abrupt shifts in
reported-result sequences and by asking whether audit discrepancies concentrate
by scanner, batch, or source. They do not prove fraud, certify correctness, or
replace risk-limiting audits. This paper instantiates the W.01 forensic report
contract for two sequence-oriented analytic families: change-point detection on
RCOUNT status-event and batch-order series, and concentration tests over
V-series audit observation discrepancies. The contribution is a method
framework and report shape, not shipped software: no forensics crate, code
fixture, function, or test is claimed here. The methods are established, the
contract is defined in W.01, and the fixtures described below are intended
roles for later implementation.
\end{abstract}
