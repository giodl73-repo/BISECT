\section{Conclusion}

Batch and scanner effects are useful forensic signals because many election
records are organized by operational grouping. They are also easy to overread.
This paper defines the W.03 method framework for treating those effects as
investigative, group-level deviations: model vote share or residuals with a
batch/scanner factor, estimate group-level effects, rank groups relative to
within-group and baseline variation, and report the result through the W.01
contract.

The contribution is not a shipped implementation. No forensics crate, fixture,
function, or test is asserted here. The contribution is the method boundary and
report discipline: bind the analysis to RCOUNT source hashes, attribute the
score to the correct grouping, disclose the baseline, and state plainly that the
output is an investigation queue rather than proof or certification.
