\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The active real ensemble package measures unweighted tract-graph cut fraction,
not Polsby--Popper, Reock, perimeter, or the geographic boundary-length
objective used by the BISECT benchmark. Rhode Island, Iowa, and North Carolina
show low cut-fraction percentiles, but Iowa and North Carolina lack the ESS
needed for precise tail claims and the two implementations disagree materially.
No polygon compactness distribution was generated. The prior 61st--72nd
Polsby--Popper percentile tables and compactness--partisan correlations are
therefore withdrawn as empirical findings.
\end{abstract}
