\section{Implemented Surface}

The core verifier names the ranked-choice design check
\texttt{verify\_ranked\_choice\_audit\_design}. It is called for every
\texttt{AuditAlgorithmRun}. For non-ranked methods, the check rejects stray
\texttt{rcv\_elimination\_order} values or nonempty
\texttt{ranked\_choices}; ranked evidence must not leak into unrelated audit
methods. For \texttt{RAIRE\_IRV\_METHOD\_ID} and
\texttt{AWAIRE\_IRV\_METHOD\_ID}, whose string values are
\texttt{raire-irv-v1} and \texttt{awaire-irv-v1}, the check requires a ranked
surface rather than a plurality surface.

The ranked method must have at least two candidates in the elimination order,
at least one assertion, and every assertion must have kind
\texttt{RankedChoiceAssertion}. The elimination order cannot contain duplicate
candidates. Each sample step must carry nonempty ranked choices, and each choice
must name a candidate from the elimination order without duplication within the
sampled ranking. The generic audit-run verifier then continues with ordinary
assertion id, sample-step id, rational-value, and source-reference checks.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Check & Failure caught \\
\midrule
\texttt{is\_supported\_audit\_algorithm\_method} & Unknown method ids before ranked-choice design is considered. \\
\texttt{verify\_ranked\_choice\_audit\_design} & Missing or duplicate elimination-order candidates, missing ranked assertions, wrong assertion kind, missing ranked choices, unknown ranked choices, and duplicate choices within one ranking. \\
\texttt{audit\_algorithm\_transcript} & Accepted boundary records for RAIRE and AWAIRE method identity and evidence shape. \\
\texttt{replay\_audit\_algorithm\_statistics} & Boundary transcripts stating that RAIRE assertion replay and AWAIRE adaptive replay are recorded but not statistically replayed. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

In \texttt{rcount-audit}, \texttt{replay\_audit\_algorithm\_statistics}
dispatches both ranked methods to boundary transcripts. The RAIRE message says
that IRV assertion replay is recorded but not replayed. The AWAIRE message says
that IRV adaptive replay is recorded but not replayed.
