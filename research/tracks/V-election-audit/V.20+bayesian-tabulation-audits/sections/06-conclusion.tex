\section{Conclusion}

RCOUNT V.20 adds a narrow Bayesian tabulation audit boundary. The method id
\texttt{bayesian-tabulation-audit-v1} is supported, the Bayesian-only fields
are excluded from other audit methods, and a Bayesian run must carry non-empty
prior and likelihood identifiers, bounded posterior probability fields, a valid
optional draw count and calibration threshold, and at least one
\texttt{bayesian-outcome} assertion.

That is a useful but modest contract. It makes posterior audit analytics public
and structurally verifiable, and it prevents those analytics from being
misreported as frequentist risk-limiting replay. The remaining work is the work
named in the plan: a tiny hand-computable posterior fixture and a calibrated
risk boundary fixture. Until then, the implemented surface should be read as a
record-shape and reporting-safety contract, not as a full Bayesian audit engine.
