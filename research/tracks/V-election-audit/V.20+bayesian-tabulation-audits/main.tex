\begin{abstract}
Bayesian tabulation audits, in the Rivest-Shen tradition, estimate the posterior
probability that a full hand count would overturn the reported outcome. Unlike
frequentist risk-limiting audits, they report an upset probability under a
prior rather than a distribution-free risk limit. This paper documents the
RCOUNT V.20 boundary contract for that surface. The implemented verifier does
not run a posterior simulation. It verifies that a declared Bayesian audit
record is shaped as Bayesian evidence, that prior and likelihood identifiers are
present, that declared posterior winner and upset-risk fields are bounded, and
that any calibrated risk limit is only a declared field unless a separate
calibration rule is implemented. The result is a deliberately narrow contract:
posterior analytics can be recorded and replayed as public evidence without
being confused with risk-limiting certification.
\end{abstract}
