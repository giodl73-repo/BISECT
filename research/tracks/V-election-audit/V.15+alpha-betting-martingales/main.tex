\begin{abstract}
ALPHA treats a risk-limiting audit as a test-supermartingale for a bounded-mean
assertion. Rather than trusting a reported risk value, RCOUNT replays the
published assorter values and betting parameters, recomputes the nonnegative
martingale in exact rational arithmetic, converts the running statistic to a
parts-per-million $p$-value, and checks the declared stop-or-continue decision.
This paper documents the implemented V.15 slice: a single-assertion,
with-replacement, fixed-bet bounded-mean martingale exposed through
\texttt{alpha-martingale-v1}. The claim is narrow but useful: when the public
audit record declares the assorter, upper bound, risk limit, ordered sample
values, and per-step bets, an independent verifier can regenerate the ALPHA
transcript from package evidence.
\end{abstract}
