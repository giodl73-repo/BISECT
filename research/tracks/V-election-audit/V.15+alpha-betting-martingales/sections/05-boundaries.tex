\section{Boundaries}

The claim boundary is the central V.15 point: a martingale value alone is not an
independently replayable audit claim. The transcript must record the betting
rule or, in the current implementation, the actual bet sequence. It must also
record the assorter identity, the assertion upper bound, the sampling mode, and
the draw order. Without those fields, RCOUNT can preserve the evidence but
cannot regenerate the statistical claim.

The implemented recurrence is a bounded-mean betting martingale. It is not a
BRAVO ballot-polling likelihood ratio, a Minerva or Athena round calculation, a
Kaplan-Markov comparison audit, a batch-comparison taint product, or a
stratified combined-risk calculation. Those methods have separate method ids or
separate boundary records in the audit replay dispatcher.

The bet-selection boundary is equally important. ALPHA can support adaptive
betting strategies, but the current replay does not infer such a strategy from
the observations. It verifies the declared transcript: each per-step bet must
be present, and the verifier recomputes what follows from that bet. A future
adaptive fixture must therefore declare enough deterministic bet-selection state
for the same replay principle to hold.

Finally, this slice does not prove sample selection, ballot custody, CVR
interpretation, or legal compliance. It assumes the ordered sample stream and
assorter values are the public evidence being checked by this method. Sampler
replay, summary reconciliation, and source hashing remain separate RCOUNT
contracts.
