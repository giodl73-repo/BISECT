\section{ALPHA Contract}

The audit record carries the public inputs the ALPHA replay needs:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Field & Meaning \\
\midrule
\texttt{method\_id} & Versioned method name. Current value: \texttt{alpha-martingale-v1}. \\
\texttt{risk\_limit\_ppm} & Risk limit in parts per million. The stop threshold is $1/\alpha$. \\
\texttt{sampling\_mode} & Declared sampling mode, currently recorded as package evidence while the implemented replay exercises the fixed-bet bounded-mean path. \\
\texttt{assertions} & The implemented replay requires exactly one bounded-mean assertion. \\
\texttt{assertion\_id} & Identifier binding each sample step to the assertion under test. \\
\texttt{assorter\_id} & Identifier for the function that maps sampled evidence to a bounded value. \\
\texttt{assorter\_upper\_bound} & Upper bound $u$ for every declared assorter value. \\
\texttt{sample\_steps} & Ordered sample stream. \\
\texttt{assorter\_value} & Rational observation $x_i$ for a sample step. \\
\texttt{bet} & Rational betting parameter $\lambda_i$ used at that step. \\
\texttt{statistic} & Optional declared martingale value to check against replay. \\
\texttt{p\_value\_ppm} & Optional declared $p$-value in parts per million to check against replay. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The implemented null mean is half the declared assorter upper bound,
$m_0=u/2$. Starting from $M_0=1$, RCOUNT replays each step by exact rational
arithmetic:
\[
  F_i = 1 + \lambda_i\frac{x_i-m_0}{u}, \qquad
  M_i = M_{i-1}F_i.
\]
The update factor must not be negative, and each $x_i$ must lie in
$[0,u]$. The replayed $p$-value is the ceiling, in integer parts per million,
of $1/M_i$. The computed decision is \texttt{pass} once $M_i \ge 1/\alpha$ and
\texttt{continue} otherwise.
