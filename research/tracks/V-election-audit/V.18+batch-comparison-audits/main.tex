\begin{abstract}
Batch-comparison audits are useful when ballots are tabulated and hand-tallied
as batches rather than linked ballot by ballot. RCOUNT's V.18 surface verifies
that a sampled batch's reported subtotals reconcile to the package batch
summary, that hand-tallied subtotals are present, that the declared plurality
margin overstatement follows from reported and hand totals, and that a derived
algorithm run carries the matching overstatement taint. The replay layer then
routes \texttt{batch-comparison-v1} through the shared Kaplan-Markov taint
product transcript in integer parts-per-million arithmetic. The result is a
narrow public-evidence contract: whole-batch reported subtotals and hand tallies
must support the declared taint-product transcript, while ballot-level linkage
and legal audit sufficiency remain outside this slice.
\end{abstract}
