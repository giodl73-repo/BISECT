\section{Conclusion}

RCOUNT now has a concrete batch-comparison audit contract. Package verification
checks reported batch totals against canvassed batch summaries, requires hand
winner and loser tallies, recomputes reported and hand margins, and verifies the
declared overstatement. Algorithm-run verification then links each sampled batch
to a taint equal to overstatement divided by reported margin, while
\texttt{replay\_comparison\_taint\_product\_run} replays the resulting
\texttt{batch-comparison-v1} transcript in integer parts-per-million arithmetic.

The result is intentionally modest. It makes whole-batch comparison evidence
replayable when trustworthy batch subtotals, hand tallies, and sampled-batch
order are present. It does not infer ballot-level evidence, certify statutory
sufficiency, or hide missing public records behind a pass. Those limits are the
point: V.18 turns batch-comparison audits into a checkable public transcript
without pretending that the transcript is more granular than the evidence.
