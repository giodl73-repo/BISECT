\section{Batch Contract}

The package-level record is \texttt{BatchComparisonAudit}. It carries the fields
needed to compare one audited batch against the batch summary and hand tally:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Field & Meaning \\
\midrule
\texttt{audit\_id} & Stable id for the batch comparison record. \\
\texttt{contest\_id} & Contest whose batch subtotals are being checked. \\
\texttt{batch\_id} & Audited batch; it must exist in the package batch manifest. \\
\texttt{declared\_batch\_ballots} & Declared batch size, checked against \texttt{counted\_ballots}. \\
\texttt{winner\_selection\_id}, \texttt{loser\_selection\_id} & Pairwise plurality assertion. \\
\texttt{reported\_totals} & Reported batch subtotals for the assertion selections. \\
\texttt{hand\_totals} & Hand-tallied batch subtotals for the same selections. \\
\texttt{declared\_reported\_margin} & Reported winner-minus-loser margin in the batch. \\
\texttt{declared\_hand\_margin} & Hand-tallied winner-minus-loser margin in the batch. \\
\texttt{declared\_overstatement} & Reported margin minus hand margin. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

For the positive synthetic fixture, the reported assertion margin is five votes
and the hand margin is three votes, so the declared overstatement is two votes.
The derived algorithm run stores this as the rational taint $2/5$ in the
corresponding \texttt{AuditSampleStep.assorter\_value}. The replay surface then
passes the ordered taints to the shared Kaplan-Markov taint-product routine.
That routine validates the risk limit, requires at least one taint, rejects
taints greater than or equal to one, multiplies the running product by
$1-\text{taint}$, and emits the running p-value in integer parts per million.
