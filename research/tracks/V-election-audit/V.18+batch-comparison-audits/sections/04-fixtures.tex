\section{Fixtures}

The package-level positive fixture is
\texttt{synthetic\_batch\_comparison\_package}. It extends the mail-batch
fixture with one \texttt{BatchComparisonAudit} for
\texttt{batch:P-001:election-day}. The record declares 70 batch ballots,
reported totals of 35 for \texttt{cand-a} and 30 for \texttt{cand-b}, hand
totals of 34 and 31, a reported margin of five, a hand margin of three, and an
overstatement of two. The package passes with a
\texttt{batch\_comparison\_overstatement} check.

The algorithm positive fixture is
\texttt{synthetic\_batch\_comparison\_algorithm\_package}. It calls
\texttt{derive\_batch\_comparison\_algorithm\_run} with the sampled batch order
containing \texttt{batch:P-001:election-day}, risk limit \texttt{300000}, and a
\texttt{Continue} decision. The derived sample step carries taint $2/5$ and
source references from the batch comparison audit and its hand-tally source. The
fixture exercises both the package verifier linkage check and the CLI
\texttt{replay-audit-algorithms} path.

The derivation tests also reject an empty sampled-batch list and a sampled batch
with no matching comparison evidence. These are not statistical disagreements;
they are evidence-boundary failures that prevent a replayable transcript from
being derived.
