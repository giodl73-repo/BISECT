\begin{abstract}
Stratified and hybrid risk-limiting audits are needed when one contest spans
more than one evidence surface. A contest may have ballot-level CVRs in one
stratum, only ballot-polling evidence in another, or batch-level comparison
evidence in a third. RCOUNT now records that SUITE-style coordination layer as
a replayable structure: a stratified coordinator names its combining rule,
nuisance parameter, component runs, stratum methods, ballot counts, source
references, and risk allocations. The implemented verifier checks that the
strata are not flattened, that allocations form a full partition of the risk
budget, and that every stratum points to an existing non-stratified component
run. It deliberately reports the combined statistical risk replay as a
boundary, not as implemented math.
\end{abstract}
