\section{Stratified Contract}

The coordinator is an \texttt{AuditAlgorithmRun} whose method id is
\texttt{stratified-hybrid-rla-v1}. It is a boundary-only record: the component
runs carry their own audit method evidence, while the coordinator records how
those components are meant to be interpreted as one stratified audit.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Field & Meaning \\
\midrule
\texttt{method\_id} & Versioned coordinator name. Current value: \texttt{stratified-hybrid-rla-v1}. \\
\texttt{combining\_rule\_id} & Declared rule for combining independent stratum evidence, such as the synthetic \texttt{suite-nuisance-boundary-v1} fixture rule. \\
\texttt{nuisance\_parameter} & Rational nuisance value or optimization transcript anchor required by the declared design. \\
\texttt{risk\_limit\_ppm} & Contest-level risk limit, stored in parts per million when present. \\
\texttt{strata} & Ordered stratum records. The implemented verifier requires at least two strata. \\
\texttt{decision} & Boundary decision for the coordinator until combined-risk replay is implemented. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Each stratum record carries the public link from the coordinator to one
component audit run:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Field & Meaning \\
\midrule
\texttt{stratum\_id} & Stable, nonempty stratum name. Stratum ids must be unique within the coordinator. \\
\texttt{method\_id} & Non-stratified audit method used by the component run. \\
\texttt{component\_run\_id} & Existing audit algorithm run that supplies the stratum-level evidence. \\
\texttt{ballot\_count} & Optional stratum sampling-frame size; if present it must be positive. \\
\texttt{allocation\_ppm} & Required positive allocation share. The coordinator allocations must sum to \texttt{1000000}. \\
\texttt{source\_refs} & Optional evidence references for the stratum. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The partition rule implemented today is therefore structural. The strata must
remain distinct, must refer to existing component runs, must not recursively
name another stratified coordinator as their method, and must allocate the
entire one-million-parts risk budget. The statistical combination is preserved
as declared metadata rather than recomputed.
