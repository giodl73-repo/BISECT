\section{Fixtures}

The positive fixture is
\path{synthetic_stratified_hybrid_package}. It starts from the synthetic
batch-comparison algorithm package, adds the synthetic Minerva multi-round
ballot-polling run, and then adds a coordinator run named
\path{audit-run:stratified-hybrid-boundary}. The coordinator uses
\path{stratified-hybrid-rla-v1}, declares the combining rule
\path{suite-nuisance-boundary-v1}, records nuisance parameter $1/2$, and
sets the decision to \texttt{Boundary}.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Fixture & Role \\
\midrule
\texttt{synthetic\_stratified\_hybrid\_package} & Positive two-stratum coordinator: one batch-comparison stratum with 80 ballots and one Minerva ballot-polling stratum with 60 ballots. Each stratum receives a \texttt{500000} ppm allocation and points to an existing component run. \\
\texttt{synthetic\_bad\_stratified\_hybrid\_package} & Missing-component negative fixture. It edits the ballot-polling stratum to reference \texttt{audit-run:missing-stratum}, causing \texttt{MissingStratifiedHybridComponent}. \\
\texttt{synthetic\_flattened\_stratified\_hybrid\_package} & Flattening negative fixture. It truncates the coordinator to one stratum and gives that stratum the full \texttt{1000000} ppm allocation, causing \texttt{InvalidStratifiedHybridDesign}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The core tests check the same contract directly. The positive test confirms
that the package verifies, that the stratified run has two strata, that the
combining rule and nuisance parameter are present, and that both allocations
are \texttt{500000}. The negative tests confirm that a missing component and a
flattened one-stratum coordinator are rejected.

The audit crate adds a replay-surface test for the positive package. It finds
the \path{stratified-hybrid-rla-v1} run, calls
\path{replay_audit_algorithm_statistics}, and confirms that the transcript
status is \texttt{Boundary} with a message containing \texttt{combined-risk
replay}.
