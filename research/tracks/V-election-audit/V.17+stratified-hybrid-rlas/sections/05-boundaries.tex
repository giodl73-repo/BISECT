\section{Boundaries}

The V.17 claim boundary is narrow. RCOUNT preserves the stratum structure,
component run links, combining-rule id, nuisance parameter, and allocation
partition. It rejects malformed structures that would make a hybrid audit look
like one homogeneous sampler or one unsupported statistical run.

It does not yet replay the combined statistical risk. The plan states this
directly: the combined risk claim depends on correct stratum definitions and
sampling frames, and RCOUNT now preserves strata and component run references,
but still does not claim combined-risk replay. The audit crate mirrors that
boundary by returning a method-specific boundary transcript instead of a pass
or fail statistical transcript for the combined risk.

This also means that SUITE, SHANGRLA/ALPHA, Fisher combination, and any
state-specific hybrid formula remain outside the implemented result unless
they are present in the component methods that already replay. V.17 is the
coordinator substrate on which those calculations can be added without losing
the public record of which ballots, batches, or jurisdictions belonged to
which evidence surface.
