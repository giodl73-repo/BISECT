\section{Conclusion}

RCOUNT now has a replayable structure contract for stratified and hybrid RLAs.
The verifier recognizes \texttt{stratified-hybrid-rla-v1}, requires a declared
combining rule and nuisance parameter, verifies that at least two strata remain
distinct, checks that each stratum points to an existing non-stratified
component run, and requires allocation shares to form a complete
\texttt{1000000} ppm partition.

The implementation is intentionally understated. It protects the public audit
record from missing components and flattened-stratum misuse, and it exposes the
method through algorithm replay as a documented combined-risk boundary. The
next statistical layer can implement the selected SUITE or hybrid combination
rule on top of that structure; until then, RCOUNT claims structure
verification, not combined-risk replay.
