\begin{abstract}
Minerva is a round-based ballot-polling risk-limiting audit: it groups sampled
ballots into audit rounds, recomputes a binomial-tail likelihood ratio from
cumulative observations, and stops when the round statistic reaches the risk
limit threshold. Athena is the related public-method surface that RCOUNT now
records without claiming risk replay. This paper documents the RCOUNT
Minerva/Athena contract. A verifier reads the method id, reported two-candidate
totals, risk limit, optional round boundaries, and ballot observations from an
audit record; routes Minerva records to the exact round replay in
\texttt{rcount-stats}; and emits an explicit boundary transcript for Athena or
for Minerva records missing required inputs. The claim is one layer below the
statistical policy question: when sufficient public evidence exists, the
declared round transcript is reproducible from the declared record.
\end{abstract}
