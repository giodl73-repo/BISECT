\section{Implemented Surface}

The algorithm dispatcher in \texttt{rcount-audit} routes
\texttt{minerva-ballot-polling-v1} to \texttt{replay\_minerva\_run}. That
function requires \texttt{risk\_limit\_ppm}, \texttt{reported\_winner\_votes},
\texttt{reported\_loser\_votes}, and at least one sample step before it calls
\texttt{replay\_minerva\_ballot\_polling\_rounds} in \texttt{rcount-stats}.
The statistics crate owns the cumulative round replay and returns one computed
round step per declared round.

The same dispatcher routes \texttt{athena-ballot-polling-v1} to a documented
boundary transcript: ``Athena round risk calculation is recorded but not
replayed.'' Minerva records also become boundaries, rather than failures or
passes, when required fields are missing, explicit round indices are incomplete
or decreasing, a round is empty, reported vote totals are invalid, or a sample
step contains an observation outside the implemented winner/loser surface.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Check & Failure caught \\
\midrule
\texttt{audit\_algorithm\_run} & Unsupported method id, Athena replay request, or Minerva record missing risk limit, reported winner votes, reported loser votes, or sample steps. \\
\texttt{minerva\_rounds\_from\_steps} & Partial or decreasing explicit \texttt{round\_index} values, and sample steps that cannot be reduced to winner or loser observations. \\
\texttt{minerva\_replay} & Declared round likelihood ratio, p-value, or stop decision drifts from the recomputed cumulative round transcript. \\
\texttt{cvr\_summary\_total} & Reported contest totals still reconcile to the public summaries that anchor the assertion. \\
\texttt{source\_hash\_match} & Source evidence bytes still match the declared source index. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The result is deliberately narrow. A passing Minerva transcript means that the
declared two-candidate round transcript follows from the record RCOUNT was
given. It does not expand Athena into Minerva, and it does not infer missing
round evidence from a public report.
