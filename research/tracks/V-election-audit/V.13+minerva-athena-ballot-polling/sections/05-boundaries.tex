\section{Boundaries}

This slice verifies a ballot-polling outcome assertion under the sampling and
round-planning assumptions already encoded in the audit record. It checks that
a declared Minerva stop-or-continue transcript follows from reported
two-candidate totals, a risk limit, round boundaries, and winner/loser ballot
observations. It does not verify ballot custody, voter eligibility, manifest
construction, public-seed sampling, or ballot-level CVR comparison; those are
separate RCOUNT contracts.

The method boundary is equally important. The implemented Minerva path is the
scoped two-candidate ballot-polling replay in \texttt{rcount-stats}. Athena is
currently a record-shape and method-preservation boundary only. RCOUNT may say
that an Athena audit record was carried through the package and identified by
method id, but it must not claim Athena risk replay until the required
method-specific inputs and tolerance rules are implemented.

The public-evidence boundary remains with the artifact. The Rhode Island Rep.
28 adapter records \texttt{MINERVA} metadata and emits a package-level
Minerva run, but the plan explicitly keeps that source as boundary-only until
public rows expose enough per-round ballot evidence to replay the source risk
measurement. Missing evidence is therefore reported as a boundary, not upgraded
to a trusted pass.
