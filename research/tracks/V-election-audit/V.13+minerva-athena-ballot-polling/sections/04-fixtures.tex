\section{Fixtures}

The round-one positive fixture is
\texttt{synthetic\_minerva\_round\_one\_package}. It starts from the synthetic
summary package, adds one \texttt{minerva-ballot-polling-v1} run for
\texttt{syn-2024-mayor}, reports winner and loser votes as three and one, sets
a ten percent risk limit, and supplies six winner observations with no explicit
round index. The test
\texttt{minerva\_round\_one\_algorithm\_statistics\_replay\_passes\_fixture}
checks that the replay emits one round step on sample step 5, computes the
fixture statistic, and stops.

The multi-round fixture is
\texttt{synthetic\_minerva\_multi\_round\_package}. It reuses the same sample
steps but marks steps 0 through 4 as round 0 and step 5 as round 1. The test
\texttt{minerva\_multi\_round\_algorithm\_statistics\_replays\_round\_boundaries}
checks that round 0 continues and round 1 stops, proving that the statistics
crate uses cumulative observations across explicit round boundaries.

The Athena boundary fixture is
\texttt{synthetic\_athena\_boundary\_package}. It changes the multi-round run's
method id to \texttt{athena-ballot-polling-v1}, sets the decision to
\texttt{Boundary}, and verifies through
\texttt{athena\_algorithm\_statistics\_reports\_documented\_boundary} that the
method-specific boundary text is preserved.

The drift case is covered by the CLI test
\texttt{replay\_audit\_algorithms\_exits\_one\_on\_minerva\_declared\_drift}.
It edits the declared \texttt{p\_value\_ppm} on the final round-one sample
step while leaving the observations fixed. The replay exits nonzero and reports
a declared p-value mismatch, separating a reproducible non-stop from a declared
transcript that no longer matches its public inputs.
