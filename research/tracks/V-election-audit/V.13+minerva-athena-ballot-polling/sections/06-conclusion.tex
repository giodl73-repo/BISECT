\section{Conclusion}

RCOUNT now has a replayable Minerva ballot-polling contract for synthetic
two-candidate records with sufficient ballot observations. The verifier reads
reported winner and loser totals, the risk limit, optional round indices, and
sample-step observations; recomputes each cumulative round statistic in
\texttt{rcount-stats}; and compares the declared likelihood ratio, p-value, and
stop decision against the recomputed transcript. Missing Minerva fields and all
Athena records produce explicit method-specific boundaries.

This extends the BRAVO paper by moving from a per-draw sequential transcript to
a round-based transcript. The statistical machinery differs, but the public
contract is the same: a named audit method should be either replayed from its
declared evidence or bounded as unreplayable, never silently trusted.
