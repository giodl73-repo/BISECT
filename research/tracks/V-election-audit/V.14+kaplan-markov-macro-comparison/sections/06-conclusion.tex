\section{Conclusion}

RCOUNT now has a replayable Kaplan-Markov comparison contract with a MACRO path.
The verifier reads a \texttt{kaplan-markov-comparison-v1} audit run, checks that
required fields are present, recomputes the taint-product or MACRO running
P-value in exact rational arithmetic, and compares the computed transcript with
the declared per-step values and decision. Missing or inconsistent design fields
produce explicit boundary or validation failures rather than a trusted verdict.

This is the comparison-audit counterpart to the ballot-polling replay papers. It
keeps the statistical transcript reproducible while leaving custody, eligibility,
and CVR-to-ballot linkage to source artifacts and jurisdictional procedures. The
remaining work is external validation against public MACRO audit artifacts that
publish the same design inputs and ordered overstatement categories required by
the implemented replay surface.
