\section{Introduction}

Comparison risk-limiting audits are not ballot-polling audits. They do not ask
only whether a sampled paper ballot supports the reported winner. They compare a
voting system record with a human interpretation of the same sampled ballot or
batch and measure how much that discrepancy could have overstated the reported
margin.

RCOUNT treats the Kaplan-Markov comparison method as a replay contract. The
public record declares the risk limit, the ordered sampled units, and either a
sequence of taints or the MACRO design fields needed to replay the published
product shape. The verifier recomputes the running P-value and the stop decision
from those fields rather than accepting a vendor transcript.

This paper covers the implemented MACRO comparison slice of
\texttt{kaplan-markov-comparison-v1}. MACRO means Maximum Across Contests
Relative Overstatement: each sampled unit is reduced to a bounded relative
overstatement category, and the Kaplan-Markov product is updated from that
category. The claim is reproducibility of that transcript, not proof that the
physical ballot was retrieved correctly.
