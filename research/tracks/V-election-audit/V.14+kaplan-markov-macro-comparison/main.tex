\begin{abstract}
Kaplan-Markov comparison audits turn CVR-versus-hand discrepancies into
bounded overstatement evidence. In the MACRO form, the sampled unit contributes
an overstatement category and the replay updates a Kaplan-Markov product whose
bound depends on the ballot count, reported margin, and gamma. This paper
documents the RCOUNT contract for the implemented comparison method
\texttt{kaplan-markov-comparison-v1}. A verifier reads the risk limit, optional
MACRO design fields, and ordered sample steps from an audit record; dispatches
the run through \texttt{replay\_kaplan\_markov\_run}; and recomputes the declared
per-step P-value and stop decision. The contract is limited to reproducible
transcripts from declared public inputs. It does not prove CVR-to-ballot
linkage, custody, or voter eligibility.
\end{abstract}
