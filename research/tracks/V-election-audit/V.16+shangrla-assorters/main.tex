\begin{abstract}
SHANGRLA reframes many risk-limiting audits as tests of assertion means. An
outcome claim is reduced to one or more assertions; each assertion supplies an
assorter, a bounded function whose average must exceed one half under the
reported outcome. This paper documents the RCOUNT V.16 surface that has landed:
not a full SHANGRLA or ALPHA numerical replay, but a record-shape and design
contract for assertion and assorter transcripts. The verifier accepts a named
\texttt{shangrla-assorter-v1} audit run, checks assertion ids, registered
assorter ids, bounds, sample-step links, p-values, and declared assorter values,
and exposes failures through the common audit-algorithm transcript check. The
contract makes the assertion layer reproducible before later work attaches a
sequential mean test.
\end{abstract}
