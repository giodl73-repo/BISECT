\section{Assorter Contract}

The landed record surface is the package-level \texttt{AuditAlgorithmRun}. For
SHANGRLA the method id is the real RCOUNT constant
\texttt{SHANGRLA\_ASSORTER\_METHOD\_ID}, whose value is
\texttt{shangrla-assorter-v1}. The run contains one or more assertions and an
ordered set of sample steps. Each step names the assertion it belongs to and the
assorter value assigned to that sampled unit.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Field & Meaning \\
\midrule
\texttt{run\_id} & Stable id for the audit-algorithm transcript. \\
\texttt{contest\_id} & Contest whose outcome assertion is being represented. \\
\texttt{method\_id} & Versioned algorithm id; for this slice, \texttt{shangrla-assorter-v1}. \\
\texttt{risk\_limit\_ppm} & Optional risk limit in parts per million, checked when present. \\
\texttt{assertion\_id} & Stable assertion id, such as \texttt{assertion:cand-a-over-cand-b}. \\
\texttt{kind} & Assertion kind, including \texttt{plurality-winner-loser} or \texttt{assorter-mean}. \\
\texttt{assorter\_id} & Registered assorter formula or function id, such as \texttt{plurality-winner-loser-v1}. \\
\texttt{assorter\_upper\_bound} & Positive rational bound for all values in the assertion. \\
\texttt{sample\_unit\_id} & Ballot, CVR, batch, or other sampled unit being assigned a value. \\
\texttt{assorter\_value} & Nonnegative rational value used by the future mean test. \\
\texttt{statistic}, \texttt{p\_value\_ppm}, \texttt{decision} & Declared audit evidence and outcome fields; shape-checked here, not recomputed as SHANGRLA. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The half-average framing is the design boundary. The assertion is written so
that the reported outcome is supported when the assorter mean exceeds one half
of its normalized scale. The current verifier checks the declared bounded values
that such a test would consume; it does not yet run ALPHA or any other
sequential test under the SHANGRLA method id.
