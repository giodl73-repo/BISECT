\section{Conclusion}

RCOUNT now has a replayable BRAVO ballot-polling contract. The verifier reads
the reported two-candidate totals and risk limit, reconstructs each ballot
observation from the declared sample steps, and recomputes the sequential
likelihood ratio in integer parts-per-million, so the published stop-or-continue
transcript can be regenerated from public evidence. Required-field gaps produce
an explicit boundary transcript rather than a trusted verdict, and the method is
exposed through the same algorithm-replay surface as the other RCOUNT audit
checks.

BRAVO is the simplest statistically meaningful step from sampled observations to
a risk-limit transcript, and it establishes the pattern that the round-based
(Minerva/Athena), betting-martingale (ALPHA), and comparison (Kaplan-Markov,
batch, SHANGRLA) methods extend. With ballot-polling replay in place, later work
can focus on the distinct statistical machinery of those methods without again
re-establishing how a declared transcript is tied back to public inputs.
