\section{Implemented Surface}

The BRAVO replay is dispatched by method id alongside the other RCOUNT audit
algorithms. When an audit record names \texttt{bravo-ballot-polling-v1}, the
algorithm-replay surface routes it to \texttt{replay\_bravo\_run} in
\texttt{rcount-audit}. That function enforces the contract before any statistic
is computed: a missing risk limit, missing reported winner votes, or missing
reported loser votes each produces a boundary transcript that preserves the
declared claim without replaying it. The same boundary outcome is produced when
a sample step cannot be reduced to a valid BRAVO observation.

Once the inputs are present, \texttt{replay\_bravo\_run} maps every declared
sample step to a winner, loser, or other observation and calls
\texttt{replay\_bravo\_ballot\_polling} in \texttt{rcount-stats}. The statistics
crate owns the sequential likelihood-ratio recurrence and returns, for each
draw, the running statistic, a risk estimate in parts per million, and whether
the audit has stopped. The audit crate then zips those computed steps back
against the declared steps to build the replay transcript.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Check & Failure caught \\
\midrule
\texttt{audit\_algorithm\_run} & Unsupported method id, or BRAVO record missing risk limit, reported winner votes, or reported loser votes. \\
\texttt{bravo\_replay} & Declared per-draw likelihood ratio, risk estimate, or stop decision drifts from the recomputed transcript. \\
\texttt{cvr\_summary\_total} & Reported contest totals still reconcile to the public summaries that anchor the assertion. \\
\texttt{source\_hash\_match} & Source evidence bytes still match the declared source index. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Because BRAVO shares the algorithm-replay dispatch, its failures surface through
the same command and transcript shape as sampler replay, stopping checks, and
the other ballot-polling and comparison methods. An observer sees the declared
and recomputed statistics side by side rather than a single trusted verdict.
