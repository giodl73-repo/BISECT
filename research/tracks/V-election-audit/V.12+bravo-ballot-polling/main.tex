\begin{abstract}
BRAVO is the classic ballot-polling risk-limiting audit: it samples paper
ballots, records whether each observation supports the reported winner or
loser, and updates a sequential likelihood ratio until the reported outcome has
enough evidence to stop or the audit escalates. This paper documents the RCOUNT
BRAVO contract, which separates the statistical claim from its public evidence.
A verifier reads the reported two-candidate totals and risk limit from an audit
record, reconstructs each ballot observation from declared sample steps, replays
the BRAVO likelihood ratio in integer parts-per-million arithmetic, and confirms
that the declared per-draw statistic and stop decision follow from the public
inputs. The contract is the simplest statistically meaningful bridge from
sampled ballot observations to a replayable risk-limit transcript, and it is
exposed through the same algorithm-replay surface as the other RCOUNT audit
methods.
\end{abstract}
