\section{Implemented Surface}

The real shared crate is \texttt{crates/rctx-core}. Its public surface includes
\texttt{RctxPackage}, \texttt{RctxManifest}, \texttt{RctxSourceIndexEntry},
\texttt{ContextUnitIndex}, \texttt{GraphRecord}, \texttt{CrosswalkRecord},
\texttt{RationalWeight}, \texttt{CrosswalkWeightKind},
\texttt{CrosswalkVerificationInput}, \texttt{ClaimBoundary}, and
\texttt{VerificationReport}. The consumer-facing functions include
\texttt{verify\_package}, \texttt{verify\_crosswalk\_input},
\texttt{package\_content\_hash}, \texttt{crosswalk\_record\_hash},
\texttt{crosswalk\_set\_hash}, and \texttt{is\_sha256\_hash}.

\texttt{verify\_package} first checks the manifest, derives the source set,
verifies crosswalk input, verifies graph context and source references, and
then verifies the claim boundary. Its reports expose named checks such as
\texttt{manifest\_package\_hash}, \texttt{context\_unit\_index},
\texttt{source\_index}, \texttt{crosswalk\_unit\_refs},
\texttt{crosswalk\_weight\_sum}, \texttt{crosswalk\_source\_refs},
\texttt{graph\_context\_refs}, \texttt{graph\_source\_refs}, and
\texttt{claim\_boundary\_present}.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
Surface & Implemented check \\
\midrule
\texttt{ContextUnitIndex} & Context hashes must be valid \texttt{sha256:} values; unit lists must be non-empty and duplicate-free. \\
\texttt{RctxSourceIndexEntry} & Source ids must be unique; source hashes must be valid \texttt{sha256:} values. \\
\texttt{GraphRecord} & Graph records must reference known contexts, carry valid graph hashes, and reference known sources. \\
\texttt{CrosswalkRecord} & Endpoint contexts and units must exist; weights must be non-negative rationals with positive denominators; duplicate rows are rejected; exhaustive groups must sum exactly to one. \\
\texttt{ClaimBoundary} & The boundary package id must match the manifest, and both \texttt{proves} and \texttt{does\_not\_prove} must be present. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The crate is already shared with sibling products. RHIST depends on
\texttt{rctx-core} for crosswalk verification, RCOUNT records RCTX references
through \texttt{normalized/rctx-refs.ndjson}, and district aggregation code can
bind RCOUNT reporting units, an RPLAN assignment, an RCTX context hash, and RCTX
crosswalk records by hash. Those uses do not make RCOUNT or RHIST owners of the
base context.

The synthetic fixture in \texttt{rctx-core} is small but representative. It
constructs \texttt{syn-rctx-l0-shared-context} with one source-index entry, one
context unit index, one graph record, two identity crosswalk rows, and a claim
boundary that proves only declared unit uniqueness, graph/crosswalk references,
and exhaustive crosswalk sums. It explicitly does not prove legal geography,
source completeness, vote totals, district assignments, or rendered maps.

Two surfaces are deliberately deferred. RMAP is not a built crate in this
repository; it is a reserved ownership boundary for future rendered map
products. A standalone \texttt{rctx-io} loader is also deferred until a second
consumer needs independent package-directory I/O rather than the current helper
and fixture shape.
