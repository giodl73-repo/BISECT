\section{Conclusion}

RCASE is a reservation for public composition. It gives the civic evidence
family a future place to assemble child packages into one citable case, but it
does not create a new source of election facts. The case layer can improve
navigation, citation, and public readability only by preserving the evidence
boundaries below it.

Keeping RCASE deferred is part of that design. Once RCOUNT, RHIST, and related
children have stable claim-boundary records, a small RCASE fixture can test the
composition surface. Until then, the enforceable rule is simpler: no new facts,
no hidden caveats, no erased conflicts, and no case-level claim that cannot be
traced back to a child package hash.
