\begin{abstract}
Public election evidence often arrives as several bounded packages: count
reconciliation, unit lineage, audit transcripts, custody records, and public
citations. RCASE is reserved for the later layer that can put those materials
into one citable case without changing what any child package proves. This paper
does not describe implemented code. It records the ownership definition, the
reason RCASE is deferred until child claim boundaries are stable, and the rule
that a case bundle may organize, cite, and summarize child claims only while
preserving child hashes, caveats, conflicts, and unsupported checks.
\end{abstract}
