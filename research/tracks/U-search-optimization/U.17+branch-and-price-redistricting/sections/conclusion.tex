\section{Conclusion}

U.17 establishes the column-generation audit contract: pricing and master
artifacts may be specialized, but selected final plans should still reach the
same RPLAN/RCTX verification path as other U-series algorithms.

This lets the implementation mature in public stages. The paper can claim a
fixture-scale contract now, while reserving large-instance exact performance for
future solver transcripts and benchmark evidence.

Under U.21, branch-and-price is a discovery and lower-bound engine for a fixed
recursive split. Its selected incumbent becomes a discovery record; separate
population, boundary, and canonical decision proofs must still be generated
and independently checked before the cut is certified.
