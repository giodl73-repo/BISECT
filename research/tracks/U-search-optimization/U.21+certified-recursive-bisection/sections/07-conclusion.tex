\section{Conclusion}

Certified recursive bisection preserves the most recognizable feature of
BISECT---a fixed sequence of proportional two-way cuts---while defining how
unproved per-node choices are replaced by a unique, checkable result. Bounded
implementation, hostile fixtures, package verification, Rhode Island's exact
population frontier, a precommitted multi-instance heuristic comparison, and
three complete national operational baselines show
that the architecture is concrete rather than aspirational.

The implemented sequential-cut ceremony makes that architecture publicly
staged: the rule is committed before the root, each later depth waits for a
review window, observers can verify disclosed prefixes without future cuts,
and a challenge can preserve a permanently halted record. The bounded path-8
fixture exercises a ceremony-enabled procedural scoreboard, but externally
witnessed State ceremonies and State-scale ceremony-enabled bakeoffs remain
future evidence rather than completed empirical claims.

The remaining work is equally concrete: integrate the compact encoding and
pinned proof toolchain at State scale, integrate an exact discovery solver,
obtain the first independently checked unrestricted State boundary proof, and
extend the completed common-block geometry bakeoff to separately governed
election, demographic, alternative-structure, and converged-ensemble
comparisons. Until then, METIS-backed
certified discovery remains the practical nationwide engine and exact
certified BISECT remains the proof-oriented North Star.
