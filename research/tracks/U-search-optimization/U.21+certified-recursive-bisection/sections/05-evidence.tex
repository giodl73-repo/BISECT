The bounded corpus establishes contract correctness, identity binding, and
tamper resistance. The exact reference oracle remains bounded, but the
operational certified-discovery path now establishes State-scale execution,
complete block coverage, connected recursion, and independent package
verification. These are distinct evidence layers: operational verification
does not convert a discovered incumbent into an exact objective proof.

The State-scale frontier now goes one step further. Deterministic METIS plus
nine articulation-safe boundary moves reaches Rhode Island populations
548,689 and 548,690. RoundingSat proves that deviation below 1 is impossible,
and VeriPB accepts that proof. The boundary decision timed out, so the root cut
is not yet fully certified.

Across the three national cycles, all 1,155 recursive nodes satisfy the frozen
population tolerance. Thirteen nodes attain the arithmetic lower floor. Exact
weighted-boundary and canonical proof coverage remains 0 of 1,155. The
structural comparison finds 120 node signatures common to all three cycles and
18 States with exact all-cycle tree topology. Pairwise topology Jaccard is
0.328 for 2000--2010, 0.283 for 2000--2020, and 0.360 for 2010--2020.
Cross-census assignment overlap is deliberately uncomputed because changing
Census block universes require a relationship crosswalk or geometry overlay.

The strongest demonstrated comparative result therefore remains epistemic:
certified BISECT can state what would count as proof, distinguish SAT
counterexamples from UNSAT certification requests, bind the final plan to the
recursive evidence tree, and separate national operational completion from
exact-objective coverage. Nationwide political and map-quality comparisons
remain future empirical work.
