\section{Synthetic Reporting Template}
\label{sec:results}

This section contains no empirical survey results. The numerical values in the
earlier draft---including $0.41$ SD, $0.22$ SD, $0.18$ SD, $p$-values, support
percentages, balance statistics, and robustness checks---were synthetic worked
examples and are withdrawn as findings.

A completed study will report:

\begin{enumerate}
\item respondent flow from invitation through the analytic sample;
\item condition counts and deviations from the registered stopping rule;
\item primary contrasts with effect sizes, confidence intervals, exact
$p$-values, and multiplicity adjustments;
\item panel-source heterogeneity and preregistered subgroup estimates;
\item measurement reliability and manipulation-check results;
\item null, adverse, and unexpected findings;
\item code and de-identified data sufficient to reproduce every table; and
\item sensitivity to weights, exclusions, clustering, and missing-data rules.
\end{enumerate}

Each respondent is expected to rate two maps, so a target of $2{,}400$
respondents may produce up to $4{,}800$ respondent-map observations. These are
different quantities and must be labeled separately in every table.

No coefficient, confidence interval, partisan difference, or policy-support
percentage may be inserted into this section before the registered analysis is
executed on archived observed data.
