\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper defines a survey protocol; it does not establish that the public
prefers algorithmic maps, that transparency increases perceived fairness, or
that support crosses partisan lines.

The protocol can become an empirical contribution only after public
registration, ethics review, recruitment, data collection, reproducible
analysis, and release of the permitted replication materials. Until those
gates pass, C.6 may be cited for its proposed design and interpretation rules,
not for any numerical or policy finding.
