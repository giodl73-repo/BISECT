# NRS v0.3 Initial DFS Partition Census Protocol

**Protocol ID:** `nrs-v0.3-initial-dfs-partition-census-v1`
**Status:** precommitted before partition instrumentation and execution
**Date frozen:** 2026-08-08
**Owner:** BISECT reference-engine and research evidence layers

## Frame

The initial DFS candidate census preserved all 44 governed 2020 State roots
and reported 29 roots with two minimum-deviation/minimum-cut candidates.
Every flagged root has an equal child-seat split, and no root reported more
than two such candidates.

Source inspection shows that each tree-edge cut is evaluated in both
left/right orientations. For equal child-seat splits, those two orientations
have identical population deviation and boundary cut even though the seeded
moved-population tie-break may choose their labels differently.

The falsifiable thesis is:

> Collapsing complementary orientations will distinguish multiple physical
> tree-edge bipartitions from the automatic two-orientation symmetry of one
> bipartition without changing any governed assignment or objective.

The thesis fails if the new counter changes candidate selection, is not
deterministic, exceeds the oriented candidate count, or cannot be exactly
regenerated for all governed roots.

## Instrumentation Contract

For candidates tied at minimum population deviation and minimum weighted
boundary cut, construct each candidate's binary unit-membership vector and
normalize it so unit zero is always labeled zero. Count distinct normalized
vectors.

Append the count to the discovery method string as:

`initial-dfs-minimum-deviation-cut-partitions=<n>`

The existing oriented counters and selection key remain unchanged. In
particular, moved population relative to the seeded METIS assignment and
minimum unit index continue to choose the labeled assignment.

## Frozen Census

- Census year: 2020.
- Universe: all 44 governed multi-district State roots.
- Inputs, benchmark seeds, refinement, and acceptance checks: unchanged from
  `nrs-v0.3-initial-dfs-tie-census-v1`.
- One instrumented benchmark-seed replay per State.
- No State may be added, removed, or replaced after outcomes.

The package must report oriented minimum-cut candidate count, distinct
unlabeled partition count, child-seat equality, and exact assignment/objective
preservation for every State.

## Decision Rule

- A partition count above one identifies competing physical initial DFS cuts
  and permits a separately precommitted seed-sensitivity sample.
- A partition count of one with an oriented count of two identifies only
  left/right orientation opportunity at this stage.
- If all 44 roots have one partition, stop physical-cut sensitivity expansion
  at the initial root-0 DFS stage. Any later work must separately justify
  label-orientation, child-node, or fallback sensitivity.

## Claim Boundary

This census observes unlabeled initial root-0 DFS tree-edge bipartitions only.
It does not prove seed-invariant labels, full recursive plans, child nodes,
alternate-root or bridge-aware fallback behavior, national robustness,
optimality, or legal quality.
