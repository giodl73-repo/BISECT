# NRS v0.3 NH/NM/GA Block-Ensemble Expansion v2

**Protocol ID:** `nrs-v0.3-block-ensemble-expansion-v2`

**Frozen:** 2026-08-11, before implementation preflight or governed v2 chains

**Status:** protocol frozen; implementation, review, and Stage 0 gates remain;
execution is not authorized by this document alone

## Relationship To The Closed v1 Protocol

`nrs-v0.3-block-ensemble-expansion-v1` remains failed and closed. Its five
completed primaries, terminal GA Kruskal disk-full record, empty governed
replay set, ledger, and claim boundary are immutable. They cannot count toward
this protocol, be copied into its package, or be described as v2 draws.

Version 2 is a new complete experiment with a fresh seed stream, a fresh
package, and a fresh all-or-nothing decision. It addresses the same bounded
three-State feasibility and kernel-sensitivity question; it is not a retry,
resumption, repair, or completion within the v1 protocol.

The cohort, inputs, population tolerance, kernels, chain count, chain length,

## Fresh Seed And Schedule

The v2 base seed is `20260811`, selected mechanically from the protocol freeze
date before any v2 process. No v1 metric or chain outcome selected this seed.
Use `bisect_ensemble::chain::chain_seed` for four chains per State and kernel.

Each governed chain runs 2,000 steps, discards the first 500 steps for
analysis, records every step, and snapshots every 10 steps at population
tolerance `0.005`. Execute one process and one chain at a time in exactly this
order:

1. NH Wilson, then NH Kruskal;
2. NM Wilson, then NM Kruskal; and
3. GA Wilson, then GA Kruskal.

Complete all six primaries before any replay. Then replay all six in the same
order. A failed, invalid, or non-converged chain cannot be replaced, extended,
or rerun under another seed or parameter. A valid non-converged result is
retained as negative evidence.

## Resource And Host-Capacity Contract

The Pulse 24 mechanically derived ceilings remain fixed:

- 21 cumulative runner hours across the six primaries and six replays;
- 2,415,919,104 bytes peak RSS per runner process;
- 3 GiB retained evidence; and
- 3 GiB replay scratch evidence.

The Pulse 25 observations are diagnostic only: five successful primaries used
21,505.88 runner seconds, the terminal process ended after 602.30 seconds, and
the largest observed peak RSS was 1,401,757,696 bytes. These observations do
not raise or lower the frozen ceilings.

Immediately before every preflight, primary, and replay process, invoke
`nrs-block-ensemble-admitted-launch-v1` against the v2 package and ledger. With
zero retained custody, admission requires 8 GiB free: 3 GiB scratch, 3 GiB
remaining retained capacity, and 2 GiB host safety reserve. Retain one unique,
non-overwriting admission record per process.

A prelaunch rejection creates no runner process and is not a chain failure.
Operators may remove only independently disposable data and repeat admission.
Once a runner starts, any nonzero exit, resource-ceiling breach, invalid trace,
schedule violation, or replay mismatch closes v2 without retry. The capacity
adapter does not replace the enforcing resource monitor.

## Stage 0 And Execution Gate

No v2 chain may run until all of the following are committed and reviewed:

1. a dedicated v2 runner and ledger schema that cannot read v1 completions;
2. tests for the exact seed, parameters, order, fresh-package boundary,
   capacity-admission requirement, and terminal failure semantics;
3. a verifier that rejects v1 protocol IDs, traces, ledgers, and admission
   records in the v2 package;
4. exact re-verification of the NH/NM/GA input audits and executable custody;
5. one excluded 25-step preflight per State and kernel in frozen order; and
6. a fresh-process exact normalized replay of all six preflights.

Stage 0 records timing, memory, trace size, acceptance, tolerance, and replay
diagnostics, but its samples are barred from governed analysis and stopping
decisions. If Stage 0 fails, no governed chain runs.

## Diagnostics, Verification, And Decision

Analyze each State and kernel separately under the frozen v1 definitions:

- split-chain R-hat for cut fraction and weighted boundary cut;
- per-chain and pooled ESS for both metrics;
- acceptance and maximum population deviation;
- label-aligned Hamming-distance diagnostics from snapshots; and
- descriptive cross-kernel means, standard deviations, quantiles, and KS.

A kernel is labeled converged only when both scalar metrics have split-R-hat
below 1.05 and pooled ESS at least 100. Tail statements outside 1--99 percent
require relevant ESS at least 1,000. Kernels and States may not be pooled.

The v2 gate passes only if all 24 primary chains complete, every recorded plan
is contiguous and within tolerance, all resource and admission records pass,
analysis recomputes, and the six fresh replays regenerate normalized metrics
and canonical snapshots exactly. Otherwise it fails and closes.

## Custody And Claim Boundary

All v2 artifacts live under
`docs/experiments/nrs-v0.3-block-ensemble-expansion-v2/`. The package must bind
the protocol, runner, verifier, executable, inputs, ledgers, admission records,
resource records, traces, analysis, and manifest by SHA-256. It must preserve
v1 only by reference to its immutable path and terminal status.

A passing package may support only 2020 NH/NM/GA block-graph feasibility,
registered scalar diagnostics, deterministic replay, and State-specific
Wilson-versus-Kruskal sensitivity under the frozen starts and v2 seeds. It
cannot establish national representativeness, mixing, sampler equivalence,
neutrality, partisan or demographic fairness, VRA compliance, legal validity,
polygon compactness, or coverage of all valid plans.
