# NRS v0.3 Fallback Candidate Census Protocol

**Protocol ID:** `nrs-v0.3-fallback-candidate-census-v1`
**Status:** precommitted before fallback candidate instrumentation
**Date frozen:** 2026-08-08
**Owner:** BISECT reference-engine and research evidence layers

## Frame

The cross-census complete-tree replay reduced the governed fallback universe
from 1,155 nodes across three Census cycles to seven v0.2 activations and one
v0.3 activation. All 1,155 nodes had one physical initial root-0 DFS cut.

The remaining missing capability is observability inside the activated
fallback selection stages, where moved population relative to the seeded METIS
assignment can break candidates tied first on population deviation and
weighted boundary cut.

The falsifiable thesis is:

> Behavior-preserving fallback diagnostics can determine whether any activated
> governed node presents more than one physical candidate at the point before
> the seed-dependent moved-population criterion.

The thesis fails if diagnostics change a governed assignment or objective, if
the frozen candidate universes are not measured exactly, or if any activated
node cannot be replayed.

## V0.2 Candidate Universe

For each activated v0.2 node, observe:

1. the v0.1 incumbent entering fallback; and
2. each candidate from the distinct nonzero roots in the frozen 16-quantile
   alternate-root schedule, after equal-seat orientation normalization and the
   existing population repair.

Report:

- evaluated candidate count;
- minimum-deviation candidate count;
- minimum-deviation/minimum-cut candidate count; and
- distinct unlabeled partitions among the minimum-deviation/minimum-cut
  candidates.

The existing selection key remains deviation, cut, moved population, and
canonical assignment.

## V0.3 Candidate Universe

For the activated v0.3 node, observe only candidates the current algorithm
actually compares:

1. the v0.2 incumbent;
2. the first feasible raw bridge-aware candidate selected by the frozen
   traversal, when present; and
3. that candidate after the existing population repair.

The same four metrics are reported. This is not an exhaustive census of every
bridge-aware construction because the current algorithm intentionally stops
at the first feasible population-floor candidate.

## Decision Rule

Only a stage with more than one distinct unlabeled
minimum-deviation/minimum-cut fallback partition advances to a separately
precommitted seed sensitivity slice. Otherwise stop fallback seed expansion at
the governed benchmark candidates.

## Claim Boundary

This census measures candidates evaluated by the current fallback algorithms
at eight activated stage/node pairs. It does not prove seed-invariant
candidate generation, labels, complete plans, robustness, optimality,
partisan properties, or legal quality.
