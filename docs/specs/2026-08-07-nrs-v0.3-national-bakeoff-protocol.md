# NRS v0.3 National Bakeoff Protocol

**Protocol ID:** `nrs-v0.3-national-bakeoff-v1`  
**Status:** Tiers 1 and 2 executed; input-semantic amendments preserved in the deviation ledger
**Date frozen:** 2026-08-07  
**Owner:** BISECT research evidence layer

## Frame

The governed NRS v0.3 packages already provide complete, independently
verified operational assignments for the 2000, 2010, and 2020 Census cycles.
Those packages remain authoritative and are not modified by this experiment.

The missing capability is a hash-bound post-hoc comparison layer that locates
NRS assignments relative to enacted plans, alternative BISECT structures, and
valid ensembles without importing comparison data into the NRS generator.

The falsifiable V1 thesis is:

> A plan-family comparison can place NRS v0.3 on common-unit assignment and
> subdivision metrics using frozen sources and outcome-blind rules, while
> preserving explicit unavailable states for geometry, elections,
> demographics, sensitivity, and ensembles.

The thesis fails if the comparison cannot construct a common atomic-unit
universe without an outcome-selected allocation rule, if source hashes do not
reproduce, or if the same metric definition produces materially different
results under equivalent transport formats.

## Ownership And Non-Goals

- NRS generation owns benchmark assignments, profiles, seeds, trees, and
  operational verification.
- Each comparator owns its source plan, generation method, and legal status.
- This package owns only source binding, common-unit projection, metric
  computation, and claim boundaries.
- V1 does not rank a winner, declare fairness, infer intent, establish VRA
  compliance, or treat enacted plans as an optimal gold standard.
- Missing inputs are reported as unavailable; they are not replaced with
  zeros, tract proxies, or post-outcome-selected elections.

The deletion target is the use of ad-hoc, unmanifested scripts such as
`scripts/baseline/compare_algorithmic_vs_enacted.py` for governed NRS claims.
That script is not removed until the national package covers its supported
metrics with equivalent or stronger evidence.

## Comparison Tiers

### Tier 1 - Common-Unit Plan Checks

Required before any quality comparison:

- source hashes and plan identity;
- district count;
- complete atomic-unit assignment;
- comparator projection diagnostics;
- county and tract split counts;
- optimal district-label matching;
- matched and moved atomic units; and
- explicit acceptance or structured failure.

### Tier 2 - Geometry

Polsby--Popper, Reock, convex-hull ratio, and Schwartzberg require a separately
frozen dissolve, projection, water, coastline, and perimeter protocol. Tier 1
must not emit geometry proxies.

The accepted Rhode Island slice and sequential 50-State execution are governed
by the two 2026-08-08 Tier 2 protocols. The national package covers all 50
States, both 435-district plan families, and 7,889,194 retained blocks. Its
State-weighted and district-weighted summaries are descriptive only and do not
support a winner or superiority claim.

### Tier 3 - Elections And Demographics

Partisan and opportunity diagnostics require the source hierarchy in
`docs/legal/NRS_EVALUATION_SCHEDULE_V0.1.md`: named elections, published
precinct-to-block crosswalks, aggregation and missing-data rules, and
demographic definitions frozen before outcomes are viewed.

### Tier 4 - Sensitivity And Ensembles

The 100 diagnostic seeds use the schedule's domain-separated seed formula.
Ensembles require at least four chains, frozen proposals and stopping rules,
R-hat below 1.05, ESS at least 100 for descriptive inference, and ESS at least
1,000 for sub-1-percent or above-99-percent tail claims.

## External Comparator Lessons

Current GerryChain/MGGG and ALARM/redist practice supports:

- multiple independently initialized chains;
- convergence and effective-sample-size diagnostics;
- multiple elections with published aggregation rules;
- full distributions rather than selected examples;
- multiple compactness measures rather than one dispositive score; and
- preregistration, fixed seeds, and preservation of failed chains.

This protocol is intentionally narrower at Tier 1. It adds no ensemble or
election claim until the corresponding national input and diagnostic contracts
exist.

## V1 Evidence Slice

The first slice is Rhode Island 2020, NRS v0.3 versus the committed enacted
congressional polygon source.

Frozen rules:

- NRS assignment:
  `runs/nrs-v0.3/neutral-analysis/national-2020/states/ri/package/baseline_assignments.json`;
- block geometry:
  `data/2020/tiger/blocks/tl_2020_44_tabblock20/tl_2020_44_tabblock20.shp`
  and required sidecars;
- comparator polygons:
  `data/enacted_districts/tl_2020_44_cd118.zip`, requiring
  `STATEFP20=44` and `CDSESSN=118`;
- common atomic unit: 2020 Census tabulation block with `ALAND20 > 0`;
- comparator block assignment: the block representative point must be within
  exactly one comparator polygon;
- projection for spatial predicates: `EPSG:32130`;
- district labels are matched with maximum-overlap bipartite assignment before
  moved-block counts are computed;
- county key: first five block GEOID digits;
- tract key: first eleven block GEOID digits; and
- any unmatched block, multiply matched block, district-count mismatch, or
  source-hash drift is a structured failure.

The initially named parquet was rejected because its internal `CDSESSN` value
was 116 despite the filename containing `cd118`. The rejection and replacement
rule are recorded in
`docs/experiments/2026-08-07-nrs-bakeoff-deviations.md`; no result from that
source is retained.

The accepted result reports only assignment coverage, optimal block overlap,
and county/tract split counts. Water-only blocks are excluded under the
uniform national rule below. The result does not report compactness,
population, partisanship, demographics, legal compliance, or superiority.

## Output Contract

The slice writes:

- `analysis.json`: metrics, diagnostics, status, and claim boundary;
- `manifest.json`: protocol, software, input, and output hashes; and
- `README.md`: human-readable result and reproduction command.

The verifier re-hashes the package and, when source inputs are present,
regenerates `analysis.json` in a temporary directory and requires exact JSON
equality.

## National Expansion Gate

The Tier 1 national package may run only after the Rhode Island accepted path
and at least one structured failure fixture pass. National summaries must
report both State-weighted and district-weighted results, retain every State
failure, and prohibit cross-cycle “improvement” language because Census
geographies and enacted plans differ by cycle.

For the 2020 national execution, each State uses its official
`tl_2020_<FIPS>_cd118.zip` comparator and matching 2020 tabulation-block
shapefile. Spatial predicates use `EPSG:5070` for the contiguous States,
`EPSG:3338` for Alaska, and `EPSG:3759` for Hawaii. Every State archive must
identify itself as congressional session 118 before metric computation.
The atomic universe is restricted uniformly to blocks with `ALAND20 > 0`.
Water-only blocks are excluded because official CD118 archives can classify
their surrounding water as `ZZ` ("Congressional Districts not defined")
rather than assigning it to a numbered district. Non-numeric comparator
polygons are removed before projection; any retained land-containing block
that then lacks exactly one numbered district remains a hard failure.

## Claim Boundary

This protocol can support descriptive, source-bound plan-family comparisons.
It cannot establish partisan fairness, VRA compliance, legal validity,
community preservation, causal effects, compactness superiority, exact
optimality, or official adoption.
