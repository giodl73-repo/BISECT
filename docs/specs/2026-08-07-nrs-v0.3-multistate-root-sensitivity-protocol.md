# NRS v0.3 Multi-State Root Sensitivity Protocol

**Protocol ID:** `nrs-v0.3-multistate-root-sensitivity-v1`
**Status:** precommitted before diagnostic-seed generation
**Date frozen:** 2026-08-07
**Owner:** BISECT research evidence layer

## Frame

The Rhode Island 100-seed slice reproduced one assignment because that root
did not exercise a seed-sensitive moved-population tie. The governed national
NRS v0.3 assignments remain authoritative.

The missing evidence is a cross-State root sample that exercises equal and
unequal seat schedules plus roots that required connected-subtree population
improvement.

The falsifiable thesis is:

> The frozen New Hampshire, New Mexico, and Georgia roots can be placed in
> exact 100-seed diagnostic distributions without changing the benchmark,
> replacing failed seeds, or pooling structurally different roots into one
> unqualified robustness score.

The thesis is weakened by rejected seeds, multiple assignments, low benchmark
agreement, objective dispersion, or benchmark atypicality. Every result is
retained.

`ceil(0.005 * smaller_child_seats * parent_population)`.

## Seeds And Execution

Each State independently uses diagnostic indices 1 through 100. The byte-exact
derivation is unchanged from
`nrs-v0.3-ri-sensitivity-v1`:

`SHA-256(ASCII("NRS_SENSITIVITY_V1") || 0x00 || canonical_manifest || uint32_be(i))`

The first eight digest bytes are interpreted as unsigned little-endian and
reduced modulo `2147483647` for the engine seed.

Every State/index pair runs `bisect exact` with:

- the frozen State context;
- the State's district count;
- method `certified-discovery`;
- refinement `nrs-v0-3`; and
- fixed timestamp `2026-08-07T00:00:00Z`.

No failed or duplicate seed is replaced.

## Metrics

For each root separately, report:

- accepted/rejected and duplicate seed counts;
- unique assignments and exact benchmark reproductions;
- label-permutation-invariant benchmark agreement per seed;
- assignment-agreement minimum, median, mean, and maximum;
- population-deviation and weighted-boundary distributions;
- population-tolerance pass count; and
- benchmark lexicographic rank interval, including ties.

The top-level package reports State-weighted and block-weighted benchmark
agreement separately. It does not pool objective values across roots because
their populations, seat schedules, and edge weights differ.

## Evidence And Verification

Each State package contains compact bit-packed accepted assignments, a
100-row seed ledger, analysis, and hashes. The top-level package binds the
three State manifests and reports every State failure.

The verifier independently checks derivations, packed assignments, metrics,
input/code/output hashes, State and top-level aggregation, then regenerates all
300 State/index pairs exactly.

## Claim Boundary

This is a three-root 2020 diagnostic sample selected for structural coverage.
It is not a national census of nodes, an ensemble, random sampling, proof of
seed robustness, or evidence about partisanship, compactness, demographics,
VRA compliance, legal validity, optimality, or benchmark replacement.
