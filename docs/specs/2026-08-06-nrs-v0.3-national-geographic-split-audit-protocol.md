# NRS v0.3 National Geographic Split Audit Protocol

**Status:** precommitted before assignment analysis
**Protocol ID:** `nrs-v0.3-national-geographic-split-audit-v1`
**Cycles:** 2000, 2010, and 2020

## Question

How many Census counties and tracts are divided among districts by each of the
three governed NRS v0.3 national assignments, under a fixed definition that can
be independently recomputed from the complete block assignments?

This is a descriptive geographic-fragmentation audit. It is not a comparison
to enacted plans and does not treat fewer splits as a legal or normative
optimum.

## Frozen Inputs

For each cycle, regenerate the governed assignment with:

- the committed national inventory for that Census year;
- the committed NRS v0.3 standard profile for that year;
- the committed NRS v0.1 legal profile used by the governed national run;
- the 50 certified State RCTX files verified by the national inventory; and
- `bisect.exe` SHA-256
  `2bcf6b13f17f237db6f755943ea1ccdac0d2e0267395c616892c6e46ce66e90e`.

Before any split result is accepted, every State must have the same raw
`baseline-tree.json` SHA-256 as the corresponding State row in the committed
NRS v0.3 national summary. Unit count and district count must also match.
Failures remain failures; no State may be dropped or regenerated with a
different profile after viewing the result.

## Frozen Geography Projection

The assignment unit is a 15-character Census block GEOID. For every assigned
block:

- county GEOID is the first 5 characters (`STATE` + `COUNTY`); and
- tract GEOID is the first 11 characters (`STATE` + `COUNTY` + `TRACT`).

The same prefix rule applies to all three cycles. The analyzer rejects a block
ID that is not exactly 15 decimal digits or whose State prefix disagrees with
the State package.

For each county and tract, retain:

- Census year and State;
- geography level and GEOID;
- sorted distinct district labels;
- distinct district count;
- number of assigned source blocks; and
- split status (`district_count > 1`).

All geographies, including unsplit and zero-population block collections, must
appear in the compact projection. District labels use the assignment package's
published one-based labels.

## Frozen Metrics

For each State, cycle, and geography level, report:

- total geographies;
- split geographies;
- unsplit geographies;
- split fraction;
- total district-geography pieces, the sum of district counts;
- excess pieces, the sum of `district_count - 1`;
- maximum districts intersecting one geography; and
- total source blocks represented.

National cycle totals are sums over all 50 States. Report both all-State totals
and multi-district-State totals. Do not average State percentages. No direct
trend claim may compare raw split counts across cycles because county and tract
definitions and the number of districts change.

## Published Evidence

The governed package must contain:

1. a row for every county and tract with its district set;
2. State and national aggregates;
3. the 150 assignment and tree transport hashes;
4. comparison to the committed State tree, unit, and district counts;
5. analyzer and independent-verifier source hashes;
6. a manifest over every published file; and
7. an explicit evaluation-readiness matrix for metrics not computed here.

The independent verifier recomputes all State and national aggregates from the
geography projection, verifies every manifest/source binding, confirms the
expected 50-State and 435-district cycle universes, and rejects headline drift.
Because the full block assignments are too large for Git, assignment-to-
projection recomputation requires the analyzer plus regenerated source files;
their SHA-256 values are therefore first-class evidence.

## Evaluation-Readiness Boundary

This audit does not compute:

- municipality splits, because no uniform, hash-bound block-to-municipality
  input has been frozen for all three cycles;
- Polsby--Popper, Reock, convex-hull ratio, or Schwartzberg, because a
  versioned district dissolve, projection, water, and perimeter protocol has
  not been precommitted;
- racial or language opportunity diagnostics, because retained nationwide
  demographic tables are tract-level and no frozen within-tract allocation
  rule may be selected after viewing district splits;
- partisan metrics, because the retained national election allocation covers
  only 2016 and 2020 presidential votes at tract/block-group resolution for the
  contiguous United States, not the evaluation schedule's two presidential
  and two House elections with a published precinct-to-block crosswalk;
- 100-seed sensitivity, because the diagnostic seed profile and execution
  package have not been run; or
- ensemble percentiles, because no four-chain precommitted national ensemble
  satisfying the schedule's convergence and effective-sample-size rules has
  been produced.

Missing inputs are reported as unavailable. They must not be replaced with
zeros, a convenient proxy, or a post-outcome-selected dataset.

## Claim Boundary

The package may claim only complete, hash-bound county and tract intersection
counts for the three named governed NRS v0.3 assignments. It cannot establish
compactness superiority, preservation of communities or municipalities,
racial opportunity, partisan fairness, VRA compliance, legal validity,
national optimality, cross-cycle improvement, or official adoption.
