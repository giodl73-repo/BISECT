# Partisan Data Postures Under The Candidate Standard

**Status:** v0.2, 2026-07-10

## 1. Benchmark generation

The statutory benchmark excludes election, party, candidate, incumbency, race,
and community signals. Its claim is limited to execution-stage input exclusion.

## 2. Final-plan legal process

The final decision maker may report partisan effects and may consider partisan
criteria where controlling law permits. Any partisan-targeting authority must
be explicit in the legal profile and modification record.

Where race-conscious and State-authorized partisan considerations affect
overlapping geography, the record must disentangle authority, inputs, stages,
decision makers, predominance, and effects. Separate optimization runs alone
do not satisfy this requirement. Partisan evidence used for racially polarized
voting analysis is not prohibited optimization input.

## 3. Research profiles

BISECT also supports or studies:

| Profile | Partisan input | Candidate federal role |
|---|---|---|
| Geographic benchmark | None | Statutory comparison artifact |
| Partisan-balanced | Target/constraint | Research, State law, or litigation alternative |
| Partisan-similarity | Edge signal | Research |
| Party-overlapping | Allocation and separate maps | Research requiring statutory change |

Research profiles must never inherit the benchmark label or its input-exclusion
claim.

## 4. Evaluation

Partisan diagnostics may include:

- Democratic seats under named elections;
- efficiency gap;
- mean-median difference;
- partisan bias;
- seats-votes curves;
- proportionality; and
- ensemble percentile under one declared profile.

No single metric defines fairness. Data, elections, uncertainty, and unit
crosswalks must be published.

## 5. Current Evidence

The real RI/IA/NC ensemble package reports 2020 presidential seat positions.
It does not support a systematic national partisan conclusion. The 2016
estimates were excluded from primary inference because they did not fully align
to 2020 tracts.

## 6. Decision Guide

```text
Generating the federal benchmark?
  Use only the statutory geographic profile.

Adopting a final congressional plan?
  Follow controlling law; publish all partisan authority and effects.

Preparing a VRA plan?
  Use the controlling VRA record and do not combine it with partisan targeting
  inside one certified optimization.

Running research?
  Use the profile required by the question and label it accurately.
```

## 7. Claim Boundary

Partisan-input exclusion does not prove partisan neutrality. A partisan metric
does not prove intent. A percentile does not determine legality. The statute's
value is precommitment, disclosure, and reviewable reasons.
